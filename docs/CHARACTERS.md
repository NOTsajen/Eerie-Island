# Island Echoes: Survivor Roster

Portrait frames to draw for every character: Neutral, Happy, Angry, Sad, Shocked, Suspicious, Scared, Exhausted, Evil.
Trust unlocks: memory fragments at 25/50/75%, heart event at 100%.

Player template: Leader, Health 100, Morale 70, Stress 50, Work 50. Passive "Survivor's Instinct": NPC sanity loss 10% slower while the leader is nearby. Seat 14C, one unnamed secret revealed through the Memory Vault.

## Ravi (M, 52) · Medic & spiritual guide
- Before crash: Temple priest (pandit)
- Look: Brown skin, grey-streaked black hair, round glasses, torn saffron kurta, tulsi beads, slight stoop.
- Perk: Calm Presence: nearby survivors recover stress 15% faster; blessing gives +10% morale for a day.
- Stats: Health 70, Morale 85, Stress Tolerance 80, Work 55
- Backstory: Flying to officiate his nephew's wedding. Lost his faith when his wife died two years ago and still performs rituals from habit.
- Memory fragments:
  - 25%: "I light the lamp each dawn. Not because I believe, but because my hands remember how."
  - 50%: "My wife asked me to pray for her. I did. She died anyway. I never told anyone I stopped."
  - 75%: "Out here, when the others kneel beside me, I feel something again. Is that faith, or only loneliness?"
- Heart event (100%): The Unlit Lamp: build a dawn shrine with him; he says his wife's name aloud. Reward: permanent morale aura + Blessed Shrine.
- Likes: Marigolds, tea, rice pudding, quiet mornings, old books
- Dislikes: Meat, loud arguments, mocking rituals
- Paranoia trigger: Unburied bodies and long nights without prayer.
- Betrayal (sanity 0%): Decides the island is cursed. Burns the storehouse and temple offerings at night and refuses to heal.
- Greetings: "Good morning! The sea is gentle today. A sign, perhaps." / "Tea is ready. Sit, sit. No one works on an empty heart." / "I found marigolds by the rocks. Small joys, yes?"
- Exhausted: "I have blessed so many... who blesses the blesser?" / "Forgive me. My words are tired today."
- Suspicious: "Someone moved the offerings. Who touches what is not theirs?" / "The island is watching us. Can you not feel it?"
- Funeral: "We return them to the earth with respect. Let me say the words, even if I am not sure who hears them."
